About this lesson

The stack is one of the most elegant ideas in computer architecture — a data structure the CPU manages automatically for us. In this 12-minute lesson, we unpack what the stack is, why it exists, and how 8085/8086 micro­processors use PUSH and POP operations during subroutine calls and interrupt handling.
