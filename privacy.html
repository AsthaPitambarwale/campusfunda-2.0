Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information we collect

CampusFunda collects the minimum personal information required to deliver study resources to you: name, email, mobile number, university, and course details. We also collect technical usage data (browser, device, pages visited) to improve the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide access to eBooks, MCQs, videos and the forum.
  • To personalise content for your course and university.
  • To send product updates, exam tips and occasional offers (you can opt out anytime).
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent misuse.

3. Sharing of information

We never sell your personal data. We share limited data only with trusted partners (payment gateways, analytics, customer support) strictly to run the platform.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for login sessions and non-essential cookies for analytics. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion any time at [email protected].

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, download, or delete your data. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
